Menus you can type in

Every menu in Relay works the same way: a row's right-click menu, the menu of a field such as Status or Assignee, a page's View menu, a tab's menu in the sidebar. Open it, just type, and press ↵.

Opening a menu​

  • Right-click an issue row, a card, a tab in the sidebar or an Inbox item. ⇧F10 or the Menu key opens the same menu for the row under the cursor.
  • Click a field: the chips under an issue's title, or a row of its Details.
  • Press a field's key on the row under the cursor in My work, Inbox or an epic: ⌥S for status, ⌥A for assignee, ⌥P for priority.

An issue's menu is the same everywhere you open it. Its fields come first, each a submenu: Status, Priority, Assignee, Epic, Sprint, Fix versions, Reporter and the rest. Then Open in tab, Open in browser, Peek, Copy key, Copy link and Ask the Assistant, and after them what belongs to the place you opened it from.

Each row shows its shortcut beside it, so a menu also teaches you the keys.

Type to search​

Start typing while a menu is open. A search field appears with what you typed, and the rows become every match, both in this menu and in its submenus. A match from a submenu shows where it lives first, quieter: Status › In Review.

  • A row matches when its name contains what you typed, anywhere in it and in any case: rev finds In Review.
  • The first match is lit, so ↵ picks it.
  • The search reaches two submenus deep. It doesn't go into Link to issue: open that one and type there.

So to move an issue to In Review you don't need the Status submenu at all: right-click, type review, press ↵.

The keys​

KeyIn a menu
↑ ↓Move between rows. Home and End go to the first and last.
↵Pick the lit row, or open its submenu. Space too, when not typing.
→Open the lit row's submenu.
←Go back from a submenu.
EscClear what you typed; then go back a level, or close the menu.
⇥Close the whole menu.

Pointing works too: resting the pointer on a row opens its submenu.

People menus​

Assignee and Reporter have their search field from the start, so you type straight away. Before you type they list you (and Assignee's Unassigned), then the people Relay knows, most seen first. Typing finds anyone in Jira, not only the people listed.

From the keyboard alone​

Change an issue's status and assignee without touching the mouse:

  1. In My work, move to the issue with ↑ ↓.
  2. Press ⌥S, type rev, press ↵. The issue moves to In Review.
  3. Press ⌥A, type tom, press ↵. It's Tom Becker's now.

Each change is a step you can undo with ⌘Z. On an issue's own page, ⌘K offers the same menus: Change status of NW-118…, Change assignee of NW-118….

The Status menu lists only the statuses Jira allows the issue to move to, grouped like the board's columns. Keyboard shortcuts lists every field's key.
