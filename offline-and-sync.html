Offline and sync

Relay keeps your issues on your computer. Everything you open comes from there at once, and Relay refreshes it from Jira in the background. To get ready for a trip without a connection, see Working offline.

Changes reach Jira in the background​

When you change an issue (move it, comment, log time), the change shows at once and waits in line to be sent. The title bar shows how many changes are on their way. Offline, they wait, in the order you made them, and go when you're back online.

Every change can be undone with ⌘Z. A change not yet sent is simply taken back; one Jira already has is changed back in Jira.

The title bar says where things stand​

The status at the top right is one of:

  • Synced: everything you changed is in Jira.
  • Syncing, with a count: changes are being sent now.
  • Offline, with a count: changes wait until you're back online. Signed out of Jira, it reads the same, and the changes go once you sign in again.
  • Not saved, in red, with a count: Jira refused a change. See When Jira refuses a change.
  • Sync failed, with a count: Relay couldn't read something from Jira (an issue, your week on the Timesheet).

Click it to see the list: each change with its issue, what changed and when, in the order they'll be sent. Click a change to open its issue; hover it to Discard it. Try now sends everything again without waiting. The same list is Changes Not in Jira… in ⌘K, and Send Changes to Jira Now does what Try now does.

Marks on what isn't in Jira yet​

A change that hasn't reached Jira is marked where it shows, with a small icon beside it:

  • A grey arrow: the change waits to be sent. Hover it to see what: "Priority not in Jira yet". Nothing to do; it goes on its own.
  • A red mark: Jira refused it. Hover it to see why: "Jira refused the priority:" and Jira's reason.

You'll see the marks on:

  • Issues in lists and on boards: beside a status or an assignee you changed.
  • The issue itself: its title, the chips under it, its description, the fields in the right-hand panel and its links to other issues.
  • Comments, before the time.
  • Logged time: in the issue's Worklogs and on the Timesheet.

Files you attach aren't marked on the issue; they wait in the title bar's list with your other changes.

When Jira refuses a change​

Sometimes Jira says no: a field the project doesn't allow, a move its workflow doesn't have, a permission you lack. The title bar then says a change was refused. Open it to see each one, marked Not saved, with what Jira said. You can try again or discard it; discarding puts the issue back as Jira has it. A refused comment or worklog with words in it also offers Copy text, so you keep what you wrote.

When someone changed it too​

Before sending a field you edited, Relay checks it in Jira. If someone else changed it since, your change isn't sent: the list shows it as Not saved, and you choose Keep mine (send yours over theirs) or Keep theirs (drop yours).

When a reply gets lost​

If the connection drops after Jira took a change but before Relay heard back, Relay checks with Jira before sending it again, so nothing is made twice.

When a read fails​

Sync failed lists what Relay couldn't read, with Jira's reason and when it last read it. Hover one for Try again, or Dismiss for something that no longer exists (an issue deleted in Jira): Relay stops asking until you refresh by hand or start Relay again. Retry Failed Syncs from Jira in ⌘K tries them all.

Refreshing​

Relay refreshes what's shown on its own. ⌘R refreshes the current page now, and Refresh Everything in ⌘K reads everything again, for the times you want to be sure. Refresh issue … from Jira in ⌘K reads one issue again.
