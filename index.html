Relay

Relay does two things Jira never did for you: it fills your timesheet from what you actually did, and it shows you only what needs you. Everything else, Jira the way you'd want it, offline and from the keyboard, comes with them.

1. Your timesheet, filled from what you actually did​

No timers. You work as you always do, and Relay proposes your timesheet from the evidence: the commits you made, the comments you wrote, the issues you moved. Each day is filled to your working hours, every draft says why it's there, and nothing reaches Jira until you accept it, one key for a whole month.

Connect your repositories and your commits count too. In Settings › Repositories, Add a repository… and pick the folder where you cloned your code (how). Relay reads its git history on your computer, never changing it, and weighs each commit toward the issue it names. Without a repository, drafts come from your Jira activity alone.

See how time tracking without timers works →

2. An Inbox of only what needs you​

Not every notification Jira sends. Every change in Jira, every issue's state and every workday is checked against your rules, on your computer. Only what a rule sends to your Inbox shows there: the mention waiting for your answer, the issue just assigned to you, the blocker that moved, the issue in progress with no update for three working days, the day you logged less than your hours. Everything else stays out of the way, kept in the issue's history, never in your list or your badge.

  • Each item says why it's there, and you act on the issue right beside it: reply, move, assign, snooze, done.
  • Rules can act for you, and ask first when they'd change Jira: the change waits in your Inbox until you approve it.
  • Quiet when you want it: quiet hours hold notifications and deliver one summary, and a morning digest starts your day.
  • The same answer every time: rules run locally, need no AI, and show what they'd do before you save them.

See how Inbox and rules work →

And Jira, the way you'd want it​

Relay is a desktop app for Jira Cloud. It keeps your issues on your computer, so everything opens at once and keeps working without a connection.

  • Change issues without waiting. Move, assign, comment and log time; the change shows at once, is marked until Jira has it, and reaches Jira in the background. Everything can be undone with ⌘Z. See Offline and sync.
  • Work anywhere. Fetch everything before a flight and keep working: reading, searching, editing, commenting, files, logging time. See Working offline.
  • See your work your way. Lists or boards, grouped and filtered as you like, and an epic's progress, pace and timeline at a glance. See Lists, boards and filters and Epics.
  • Keep notes and plans of your own. Draft issues live only on your computer, with comments and files, until you publish them. See Draft issues.
  • Find anything. ⌘P goes to any issue, your recent ones first; tabs keep the ones you're on open. See Go to issue and Tabs.
  • Do it from the keyboard. Every action is in ⌘K, every menu filters as you type, and most things have a shortcut. See Menus you can type in and Keyboard shortcuts.
  • Ask the Assistant. It answers and drafts comments, release notes and summaries from an issue and its code, with a model you choose, and recipes make your own prompts one key away. Nothing reaches Jira until you post it. See The Assistant and Recipes.

Getting it​

Download Relay for macOS, Windows or Linux from the releases page, then follow Getting started.
