Drafts

A draft is a worklog Relay proposes: an issue, a day, a start and a length. It's drawn dashed on the Timesheet, beside the time you've logged, and stays on your computer until you accept it.

Making drafts​

  • ⇧⌘G makes drafts for this month.
  • On the Timesheet, Drafts › Generate Drafts makes them for what it shows: the day, the week or the month.
  • In ⌘K: Generate Drafts: This Month or Last Month.

Making drafts again replaces that range's drafts with fresh ones, and can be undone with ⌘Z.

How a day is filled​

For each working day, Relay:

  1. Takes your hours: your working hours, less the break, less what you already logged that day.
  2. Shares them out between the issues you worked on, by what their signals weigh.
  3. Rounds to your worklog step (15 minutes by default), with at least 30 minutes per issue; the smallest fold into the larger ones.
  4. Stacks them from the start of your working day, in the order you first touched each issue.

Days off, vacations from your calendar and days you don't work are skipped. A working day with no signals at all carries over the latest earlier day whose issues are still yours and not done, and says so.

Why a draft is there​

Select a draft and Relay says why, over its issue: Drafted because: 1 commit, 1 comment, 1 status move. Signals opens the evidence:

  • the draft, its day and its length, and how sure Relay is: Strong when your commits and Jira both point at it, Medium for several signals, Weak for one;
  • when the signals came, half hour by half hour, for Jira and each repository, and the span you were active;
  • each signal itself: the commit, the comment, the move.

If the evidence is wrong, Not right? Change the issue or day… opens the draft to correct it.

When drafts go out of date​

Relay remembers what each set of drafts was made from. When you've done more since, or logged time on those days, the Timesheet says so (for example, "2 new signals and logged time changed on 1 day since these drafts were made") with Regenerate beside it.
