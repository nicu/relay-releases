Search operators

This page is made from Relay itself, so it always matches the version it documents. Type these in ⌘K or ⌘P (Go to issue). Several together must all match.

Words​

Plain words match the key, summary, description and comments, stemmed (“locations” finds “location”); the last one also as a prefix while you type. "Quoted words" match in that order.

comment:words​

The words in a comment (stemmed). With from: or by:, one comment by that person holds them.

Also written comment:, comments:.

Try: comment:"refresh token", from:me comment:deploy.

description:words​

The words in the description.

Also written description:, desc:.

Try: description:idempotency.

summary:words​

The words in the summary only.

Also written summary:, title:.

Try: summary:invoice -is:done.

field:name words​

A rich-text field, by any part of its name, holding the query’s plain words; any text when there are none.

Try: field:"Testing instructions" devtools, field:release.

People​

A name, any word of it (“pop” finds Ana Pop), or me.

from:person​

Someone commented, logged work or changed something on it. The excerpt shows their newest comment, else what they did last (logged work, a change).

Try: from:me, from:"Ana Pop" comment:review.

by:person​

Someone commented on it; unlike from:, assigning, logging work or other changes don’t count. The excerpt shows their newest comment.

Try: by:me, by:"Ana Pop" comment:review.

mentions:person​

A comment or the description @mentions them.

Try: mentions:me.

assignee:person​

Assigned to them; none for unassigned.

Try: assignee:me is:open, assignee:none.

reporter:person​

Reported by them.

Try: reporter:me.

Fields​

Names and keys match from their start: status:in finds In progress.

status:name​

Its status, by name.

Try: status:"In progress".

project:KEY​

Its project, by key.

Try: project:PAY.

type:name​

Its issue type.

Try: type:bug is:open.

priority:name​

Its priority.

Try: priority:high.

label:name​

One of its labels.

Also written label:, labels:.

Try: label:backend.

component:name​

One of its components.

Try: component:api.

version:name​

One of its fix versions.

Also written version:, fix:.

Try: version:2.4.

epic:KEY​

In an epic, by its key or words of its title.

Try: epic:PAY-776, epic:supplier.

parent:KEY​

A subtask of that issue, or in that epic.

Try: parent:PAY-12.

State​

is:state​

open (not done), done, todo, inprogress (by status category), mine (assigned to you), unassigned, watching. May be shortened: is:prog.

Values: open, done, todo, inprogress, mine, unassigned, watching.

Try: is:open, -is:done, is:watching.

has:thing​

attachment, comment, description, assignee, epic, parent, label, due, link, subtask, worklog. May be shortened: has:att.

Values: attachment, comment, description, assignee, epic, parent, label, due, link, subtask, worklog.

Try: has:attachment, is:open -has:comment.

Dates​

7d since 7 days ago, <7d before then, 2026-10-21 or today that day, 2026-10 that month, +7d ahead. Units: h, d, w, m (months), y. Also >= and <=.

updated:when​

When it last changed in Jira.

Try: updated:>7d, updated:<2026-01-01.

created:when​

When it was created.

Try: created:>30d, created:2026-10.

due:when​

Its due date; issues without one never match.

Try: due:<today is:open, due:<+7d.

resolved:when​

When it was resolved.

Try: resolved:>7d.
